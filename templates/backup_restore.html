SYSTEM RECOVERY

Backup before you change what cannot be undone.

Full-system encrypted backups preserve KOFAD Core, Market and Hubtel Direct records: business and accounting data, staff, roles and individual permission grants, products and stock, payroll, online orders, customer-service history, database-stored images/files, verified provider transactions, approved-wallet mandate evidence and refunds. Temporary login codes and active sessions are intentionally excluded. Restore accepts only an untampered backup created by this KOFAD installation on the exact deployed schema.

System administrator only
Off-site disaster-recovery status requires independent verification. KOFAD can generate encrypted full-system backups, and the company may also keep encrypted off-site copies. The existence of an archive alone does not confirm a working schedule, complete provider history, or a successful restoration rehearsal. Downloading a backup is not proof that a safe, separate copy exists. Until those controls are verified, keep encrypted copies outside Railway, store the passphrase separately, and periodically test a restore into an isolated database. Do not store confidential backup files in the public GitHub repository.

Replace the current system from a backup

Restore replaces the current Core, Marketplace and Hubtel Direct database state from a complete version-3 backup, including role groups, assigned staff permissions, customer accounts, online orders, payment evidence and product media. Restored debit mandates and pending charges are held for manual provider reconciliation, never blindly retried. Browser sessions are invalidated and you will be signed out.

RECOVERY RULES

What KOFAD protects

Server enforced

Backup files are never trusted by filename. New downloads are encrypted before leaving the server and KOFAD checks authenticated encryption, signature, checksum, record manifest and migration schema during recovery. Legacy signed plaintext backups remain eligible for compatible isolated recovery; legacy archives missing Hubtel data cannot erase an active Hubtel database. Restore and reset require the signed-in system administrator’s current password, an exact typed phrase, an explicit acknowledgement and a fresh safety backup.
