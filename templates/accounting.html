ACCOUNTING CONTROL

Automatic where possible. Controlled where judgment is needed.

Sales, purchases, returns, expenses, receivables, payables, payroll and inventory events create ledger entries automatically. Use approved manual journals for owner capital, loans, assets, depreciation, accruals, tax adjustments and other non-operational events.

These are IFRS-informed management statements. Statutory reporting still requires entity-specific policies, period-end estimates, disclosures and professional review.
