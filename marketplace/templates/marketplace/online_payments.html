Where to set up actual payouts

Paystack: open your Paystack Dashboard → Settings → Accounts to add or change the payout account. Registered-business account ownership must meet Paystack's verification requirements.

Hubtel: use your merchant account's settlement controls, or ask your Hubtel onboarding contact to link and confirm the destination. The developer API keys page does not set the bank account.

Direct bank transfers or MoMo payments outside hosted checkout need staff verification against the actual receiving account. An uploaded receipt or customer claim must not automatically mark an order paid.

Where does the money go?

  1. Customer paysPayment happens on the provider's secure checkout.
  2. KOFAD collectsHubtel credits KOFAD's collection account; Paystack records it under KOFAD's merchant account.
  3. Provider settlesYour provider pays out to the bank or mobile money destination set in your merchant account, under its settlement terms and fees.

Railway hosts the website; it does not receive customer payments. The phone number for a receipt is a contact number, not the settlement destination.

Check your actual payout destination in the merchant portal. This page does not verify or change those bank or mobile money details. A confirmed payment is not proof that a payout has reached your bank.

Before opening checkout

Hubtel needs a successful end-to-end payment test and the agreed UAT before public launch. Credentials are installed separately from the launch switch. Only verified payments update an order; an SMS or browser return alone cannot mark it paid.

Hubtel integration test evidence
