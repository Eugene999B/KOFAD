# Public website and customer policies

Policy version: 2026-10-07. The owner authorised initial operating policies on 7 October 2026.

Public pages: /about/, /faq/, /contact/, /delivery/, /returns/, /terms/, /privacy/.
Production canonical host: https://kofadimpex.com. All pages are accessible without an account.
Links from a signed-in Market account open a separate tab to preserve the shopping session.

Content lives in marketplace/public_content.py. Business contacts are taken from Company settings.
Policy launch defaults:
- Standard return request within seven calendar days; faulty/incorrect goods and statutory rights remain reviewable.
- Enquiry acknowledgement target two business days; initial return decision target three business days after necessary information.
- Approved refunds initiated within five business days after final approval/required inspection.
- Provider settlement estimate 5–10 business days after initiation, not a guaranteed deadline.
- Delivery planning estimates 1–3 business days local supported routes, 3–7 other agreed destinations; checkout coverage and fees govern availability.
- No fictional testimonials, registration claims, years of operation, customs licences or cargo insurance claims.

The return form deliberately remains available for late fault claims and manual exceptions.
Staff apply policy review; time alone does not automatically reject a customer's legal claim.
These are operating commitments and must be followed by staff, not promises of automatic processing.
Changes are prospective; preserve old versions in Git history.

Contact/feedback creates a private visitor conversation in the existing staff inbox.
Visitors do not have a chat session; staff are directed to call the supplied number.
No SMS, WhatsApp, email or public testimonial is sent automatically by this form.
CSRF, validation, a honeypot, short per-network cache throttle and recent phone submission checks reduce abuse.
